7 science fiction books like Dune

1. Hainish Cycle Series

Hainish Cycle Series by Ursula K. Le Guin: Set in a far-future universe where humanity evolved in space, this series offers thought-provoking explorations of culture, identity, and diplomacy.

2. Hyperion Cantos

Hyperion Cantos by Dan Simmons: A richly layered saga that weaves together multiple narratives, spanning religion, politics, artificial intelligence, and cosmic mysteries

3. VALIS

VALIS by Philip K. Dick: A mind-bending blend of science fiction and metaphysical exploration, where reality blurs and cosmic forces intersect with human consciousness.

4. Lord of Light

Lord of Light by Roger Zelazny: A blend of science fiction and mythology set on a distant planet, where godlike beings manipulate technology and spirituality.

5. The Expanse

The Expanse series by James S.A. Corey: A gripping space opera with intricate world-building, political intrigue, and compelling characters.

6. Remembrance of Earth’s Past Trilogy

Remembrance of Earth’s Past Trilogy (The Three-Body Problem, The Dark Forest, Death’s End) by Liu Cixin: A brilliant exploration of first contact, cosmic threats, and the survival of humanity.

7. Safehold Series

Safehold Series by David Weber: A mix of science fiction and alternate history, set on a distant planet where advanced technology is suppressed to prevent another apocalypse.
